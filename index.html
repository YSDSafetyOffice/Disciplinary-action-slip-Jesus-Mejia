Supervisor’s Description of ViolationI observed Jesus Mejia working without his safety glasses on. I reminded him to put them on and gave him multiple chances to correct it, but he continued to work without eye protection. Because he did not comply after repeated warnings, I removed him from the job site for the rest of the day.

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1926.28(a): employees must wear the personal protective equipment required for their work.
  • YSD Policy: safety glasses must be worn over the eyes at all times while on the job site.
